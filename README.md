# Winotch

Una Dynamic Island per Windows.

Una pillola nera in alto al centro dello schermo che si apre quando ci passi sopra con il mouse: musica, calendario, timer e file sempre a portata di mano.

*English below ↓*

## ⬇️ Download

**[Scarica l'ultima versione](https://github.com/Casellus/Winotch/releases/latest)**: apri la pagina e clicca su `Winotch-Setup-x.x.x.exe`.

Poi apri il file scaricato e segui l'installazione. Non servono permessi da amministratore.

> **"Windows ha protetto il PC"?** Succede perché l'app non ha una firma digitale a pagamento. Clicca **Ulteriori informazioni** → **Esegui comunque**.
> Alcuni antivirus o browser potrebbero segnalarla per lo stesso motivo.

Una volta installata, l'app **si aggiorna da sola**: quando esce una nuova versione ricevi una notifica e la installi con un clic.

> Fino alla versione 1.4.1 l'app si chiamava **Windows Island**. Se l'hai già installata, l'aggiornamento la rinomina da solo in Winotch, senza perdere impostazioni e file.

**🌍 14 lingue**: italiano, English, español, français, Deutsch, português, Nederlands, polski, Türkçe, русский, українська, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文.

# Winotch (English)

A Dynamic Island for Windows.

A black pill at the top center of your screen that opens when you hover over it: music, calendar, timer and files always at hand.

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Casellus/Winotch/releases/latest)**: open the page and click `Winotch-Setup-x.x.x.exe`.

Then open the downloaded file and follow the setup. No administrator rights needed.

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** It happens because the app has no paid digital signature. Click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
> Some antivirus programs or browsers may flag it for the same reason.

Once installed, the app **updates itself**: you get a notification when a new version is out and install it with one click.

> Up to version 1.4.1 the app was called **Windows Island**. If you already have it, the update renames it to Winotch automatically, keeping your settings and files.

## Features

- **🎵 Music**: album art, title, artist and app logo (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music…). Play/pause, next, previous, shuffle, repeat, draggable progress bar, volume of the playing app (or the whole PC), a visualizer that really follows the audio, a glow in the album's colors and a song preview when the track changes.
- **⏱️ Timer and stopwatch**: quick start (1, 5, 10, 25 minutes…). The countdown stays visible in the closed island, like iPhone Live Activities.
- **📅 Calendar**: time, date and days of the week.
- **📂 Shelf**: drop a file on the island to keep it there, then drag it out wherever you need it.
- **🎨 Customization**: size, color and roundness of the island, accent color and effects, monitor choice. Hides during full-screen games and videos and starts with Windows.
- **🌍 14 languages**: Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese.

## Requirements

Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit.

## Uninstall

**Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Winotch → Uninstall.**
